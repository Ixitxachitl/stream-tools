Ball Drop

A pachinko-style race for your chat. Viewers type !join and get a little pixel wheel in their chat color on the starting line. When a mod types !start, the trapdoors drop and everyone races down a Rube Goldberg machine built fresh for every race: ramps, flippers, funnels, loops, springs, pins and glass pipes. First wheel into the goal wins. Or switch it to 3D, where the racers are balls rolling down one long, winding track through the night sky, with a camera you can steer.

Setup guide

Get it on stream

  1. Add a Browser source In OBS click + under Sources → Browser. Paste https://drop.ixitxachitl.com/ as the URL and set it to your full canvas size, usually 1920 × 1080. The machine fills the source, and the background is transparent automatically.
  2. Connect your chat Right-click the source → Interact, move your mouse over the overlay and click the orange gear in the bottom-right corner. Type your Twitch and/or Kick channel name, add TikTok or YouTube if you stream there, and press Save & apply.
  3. Run a race Chat types !join to get on the starting line. A mod types !start (it needs at least one racer), or you press Start race next to the gear in the Interact window. Join race puts you on the line too, and New machine builds a different one for the racers already waiting.
  4. Watch it fall The first wheel into the goal gets the winner banner and fireworks. The others get a little longer to finish, the results go up, and then a new lobby opens with a new machine.
The settings window and the buttons show on stream while you're using them. Set things up before you go live, or have your mods start the races from chat.

Chat commands

  • !join: anyone. Puts them on the starting line, or in the next race if one's running.
  • !start: mods and the broadcaster. Starts the race after a 3-2-1 countdown.
  • !cam name: mods and the broadcaster, in the 3D view. Follows that racer; !cam all shows the whole course and !cam goes back to the automatic camera.
  • All three can be renamed in the settings, so they won't clash with your other bots.

Chat sources

Twitch & Kick

Just type the channel name. Chat is read anonymously, so there's no login and no bot account needed. Mods are recognised by their badge, and a timeout or ban takes that racer off the starting line.

TikTok

TikTok needs a bridge app running on your PC. If you use TikFinity, just type 1 in the TikTok box. Otherwise, paste your bridge's ws://… address.

YouTube

Paste your live stream's video ID or link, plus a YouTube Data API v3 key from the Google Cloud Console. You can also give a bridge ws://… address instead.

What you'll see

  • A different machine every race, built from the Stunt Wheel's parts: ramps that roll the pack back and forth across the screen, peaks that split it both ways, valleys, glass coin funnels to spiral down, pinball drains with flipping flippers, and fields of pachinko pins, dice, poker chips and pop bumpers. However it's put together there's always a way to the goal.
  • Forks along the way: jumps only the faster wheels clear, trapdoors that open now and then, and glass pipes that take some wheels a few floors down in one go. Plus springs up into floating bumpers, loop-the-loops (some with a roulette wheel inside), boost pads, conveyor belts, flights of stairs, dominoes and traffic cones.
  • The floors are orange track, red riveted girders, steel coaster rails and neon tubes, hung from rods, with gears turning behind them, slot machines that hit 7-7-7 now and then, and a Vegas skyline with searchlights at the bottom.
  • Every racer's name always shows over their wheel. When they chat during the race, their emotes and emoji pop up in a bubble over their wheel; the words are left out, so the screen doesn't fill up with text.
  • A race lasts about half a minute, and the machine is the same size at any canvas resolution. Up to 40 racers by default, and common bots are ignored.

The 3D view

Set View to 3D in the settings (or add ?view=3d) for a different race: one long, wide track that winds down through space, built fresh every race, with banked turns, helixes, corkscrew loops, jumps, glass funnels to spiral down through, a pachinko slope, a pinball table with flippers, and lane splits with a boost pad down one side and a slow conveyor down the other. The racers are glossy balls in their chat colors with their initial on them.

  • The camera follows the leader by itself. In the Interact window you can drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, click a racer to follow them and double-click to go back to auto (keys: A auto, O whole course, ← → next racer).
  • It's under a night sky, so unlike the 2D view the source isn't see-through. Use it as a full scene.
  • It needs OBS's browser hardware acceleration (on by default). If it's slow, turn off Shadows.
