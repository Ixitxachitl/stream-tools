Clip Shoutout

Give another streamer a proper shoutout. When a mod, a VIP or you type !so name in chat, a comic-book card pops up with their picture, name, link and what they were last playing, and one of their clips plays in it, picked at random (never the same one twice in a row). Shoutouts given while one is on screen wait their turn, and someone with no clips still gets the card.

Setup guide

Get it on stream

  1. Add a Browser source In OBS click + under Sources → Browser. Paste https://clips.ixitxachitl.com/ as the URL and set it to your full canvas size, usually 1920 × 1080. The background is transparent automatically. Tick Control audio via OBS if you want the clips on their own mixer slider.
  2. Connect your chat Right-click the source → Interact, move your mouse over the overlay and click the yellow gear in the bottom-right corner. Type your Twitch and/or Kick channel name, add TikTok or YouTube if you stream there, and press Save & apply.
  3. Try it Test shoutout in the settings shouts you out. The box next to the gear shouts out any name you type, and Skip skips the one on screen.
  4. Shout someone out A mod, a VIP or you types !so name in chat. The card pops up, their clip plays, and it goes away when the clip ends. Give a few at once and they play one after another.
The settings window and the buttons show on stream while you're using them. Set things up before you go live.

Chat commands

  • !so name: mods, VIPs and the broadcaster. A shoutout for name (@name works too). Who can use it in the settings changes that to just you, mods only, or everyone, and you can let particular people use it too.
  • !soskip: mods and the broadcaster. Skips the shoutout on screen; !soskip all empties the queue as well.
  • Both can be renamed in the settings, so they won't clash with your other bots.
  • !so kick.com/name, !so twitch.tv/name, !so kick:name or !so twitch:name shout out a channel on the other platform.

Chat sources

Twitch & Kick

Just type the channel name. Chat is read anonymously, and channels and clips are looked up without logging in, so there's no login, bot account or app to set up. Mods and VIPs are recognised by their badges. A shoutout typed in Twitch chat shows a Twitch channel and clip; one typed in Kick chat, a Kick one.

TikTok

TikTok needs a bridge app running on your PC. If you use TikFinity, just type 1 in the TikTok box. Otherwise, paste your bridge's ws://… address. TikTok has no clips to show, so the name is looked up on Twitch (or Kick: see YouTube & TikTok shoutouts look up).

YouTube

Paste your live stream's video ID or link, plus a YouTube Data API v3 key from the Google Cloud Console. You can also give a bridge ws://… address instead. Like TikTok, the name is looked up on Twitch or Kick.

What you'll see

  • A SHOUTOUT! banner over the clip, its title in a yellow caption box, who clipped it and how many views it has, and a progress bar along the bottom.
  • A name plate with their picture, name, what they're playing (with a LIVE tag if they're live right now) or last played, their channel link, and a tagline you can change.
  • A random clip from their 100 most-viewed, never the same one they got last time (it remembers across restarts). Shorter clips are picked first, and you can stick to their top few or to recent ones.
  • A queue: shoutouts given while one is up wait their turn, with a "+2 more" tag on the card. Shout the same person out twice and they get two different clips.
  • No clips? If they have none, or the channel can't be found, the card still pops up on its own for a few seconds.
