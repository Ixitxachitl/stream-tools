Input Display

Show your controller on stream and light up every button you press, every stick you move and every trigger you squeeze. It works with any controller your computer sees: Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads, fight sticks, hit boxes, retro adapters and more. Pick a preset or build your own layout in the editor. It's all vector art, so it stays sharp at any size.

Setup guide

Show your controller

  1. Open the editor Go to input.ixitxachitl.com and press a button on your controller so the browser can see it. The dot in the top corner turns green and the preview starts lighting up.
  2. Pick a layout Click a preset on the left: Xbox Series or 360, PlayStation 5 or 4, Switch Pro, GameCube, N64, SNES, NES, Mega Drive / Genesis, Saturn, Hit Box, leverless, or a fight stick. Or start from New blank and build your own.
  3. Check it lines up Press everything. If something lights up the wrong part, click that part and press Bind, then press the right input on your controller.
  4. Choose a look Under Overlay link, pick a theme (Dark, Light, Outline, Neon or Ghost), turn on the glow, hide the controller body, or fade it out when you're not touching the controller.
  5. Add it to OBS Press Copy link, add a Browser source and paste the link into its URL field. Make it the size the editor shows under the link (e.g. 1000 × 800) or any size of that shape. Then press a button on the controller once so OBS sees it too.
Browsers only see a controller after one of its buttons is pressed. If the overlay sits still in OBS, press any button on the controller.

The editor: presets on the left, your layout in the middle, settings on the right.

Build your own

  • Add buttons, thumbsticks, analog triggers, d-pads, ball-top arcade levers, labels and shapes, then drag them where you want them. Perfect for custom hit boxes and leverless layouts.
  • Bind a part by pressing it: click Bind, then press the button or move the stick. Use + to let a second input light the same part.
  • The bar under the canvas shows every button and axis your controller sends, so odd adapters and DirectInput sticks are easy to set up. The All inputs preset shows the lot.
  • Give each part its own colors and shape, mirror parts to the other side, snap to a grid, and undo anything.
  • Save layouts in your browser or export them as a file. The overlay link carries the whole layout, so OBS doesn't need anything saved.

Link settings

Editing the link by hand

The editor builds the link for you, but you can also tweak it yourself. Every setting is optional and missing ones use the defaults. The overlay lives at https://input.ixitxachitl.com/overlay.html.

The presets expect the standard layout most modern controllers use. A fight stick in DirectInput mode or an old adapter can number its buttons differently. If a preset doesn't line up, rebind it in the editor. Keyboard keys can be bound too, but OBS doesn't pass key presses to browser sources, so they only work in a normal browser window.
