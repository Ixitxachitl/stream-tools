Stream Pet

A little voxel critter that lives along the bottom of your stream. It hatches, eats, naps, plays with its ball and tidies up after itself, all on its own. Nobody has to press anything. As it grows up, the way your streams go decides what it turns into: there are hundreds of possible grown-up forms, and you won't know which one until it happens.

Setup guide

Get it on stream

  1. Add a Browser source In OBS click + under Sources → Browser. Paste https://pet.ixitxachitl.com/ as the URL and set it to your full canvas size, usually 1920 × 1080. The background is transparent automatically.
  2. Meet your egg Every pet starts as an egg. It hatches after about five minutes on screen.
  3. Open the settings Right-click the source → Interact. Move your mouse over the overlay and click the yellow gear in the bottom-right corner.
  4. Connect your chat (optional) Type your Twitch and/or Kick channel name, and add TikTok or YouTube if you stream there. The pet is perfectly happy without chat, but chat makes it more sociable and changes how it grows up.
  5. Save & apply Press Save & apply and close the Interact window. Your settings and your pet are saved inside this browser source.
The settings window shows on stream while it's open. Set things up before you go live, or hide the source while you tweak.

Chat sources

Twitch & Kick

Just type the channel name. Chat is read anonymously, so there's no login and no bot account needed. Subs, gifted subs, bits and raids are picked up too.

TikTok

TikTok needs a bridge app running on your PC. If you use TikFinity, just type 1 in the TikTok box. Otherwise, paste your bridge's ws://… address. Gifts, subscribes and follows all count.

YouTube

Paste your live stream's video ID or link, plus a YouTube Data API v3 key from the Google Cloud Console. You can also give a bridge ws://… address instead. Super Chats and memberships count too.

What chat does

  • Messages keep it company. First-time chatters get a wave with their name over its head.
  • A burst of chat or emote spam gives it the zoomies.
  • Subs drop cake in for it. Bits and gifts rain coins.
  • Raids bring confetti everywhere and knock it over (happily).
  • Turn on Chat commands and viewers can use !pet for hearts and !pet stats to see how it's doing.
  • Turn on Learn words from chat and it picks up chat's words and mixes them into silly little phrases (see For tinkerers for how it stays clean).

How it grows up

A life of its own

  • It gets hungry, sleepy, bored and messy, and sorts all of that out itself: food drops in from above, it sleeps on its bed, kicks its ball around, and a little broom sweeps up after it.
  • It only ages while it's on screen, so it grows up with your channel: egg, baby, child, teen, grown-up and, eventually, a wise old elder.
  • Being away is fine. When you come back it has fed itself and slept, and is just a little bored. It never comes back in a bad way.
  • Nothing dies. A very old elder lays an egg, waves goodbye and heads off on an adventure, and the new egg starts the next generation with colours a lot like its parent's.

What will it become?

That's the surprise. How chatty your streams are, how often it gets to play and sleep, the time of day you stream, and what happens along the way all add up. As a teen it shows a first hint, and the rest is revealed the moment it evolves.

There are hundreds of grown-up forms, plus a few rare ones that only turn up after unusual lives. The same kind of life leads to the same form on any channel, so you can compare pets with other streamers.

For tinkerers

Custom integrations

Streamer.bot or your own scripts can poke the pet by calling these inside the browser source:

window.pet.chat("name", "message");
window.pet.sub("name", 1);
window.pet.bits("name", 500);
window.pet.raid("name", 40);
window.pet.follow("name");
window.pet.feed("cake");   // apple, fish, cookie or cake
window.pet.play();
window.pet.stats();
window.pet.say();          // or .say("custom text")

Questions

Where is my pet kept?

In that browser source's storage. To be safe, open the settings and use Export under Your pet now and then, and keep the text somewhere. Import brings it back, even on another PC.

Does it age when I'm not streaming?

No. It only grows up while it's on screen. Hiding the source or closing OBS pauses its age.

Can it die or get sick?

It can't die. It looks after itself, and if it ever does feel poorly, medicine drops in and it's soon back to normal.

What can it learn from chat?

Only words that at least three different chatters have used, so one person can't teach it anything. Links, @mentions, anything with numbers, very long words, !commands, bots and first-time chatters are skipped, and so is anything on a public bad-words list or your own Never learn these list. It keeps at most 300 words and forgets the least used ones.

It can still put harmless words together in an unlucky order, so mods can type !pet forget <word>, and Settings → Your pet → Forget every word wipes the lot.

Can I run two pets?

Yes. Give each browser source a different Save slot.

Can I start over?

Settings → Your pet → Start over with a new egg. It asks you to confirm first.
