Duel

A water-pistol showdown for chat. When someone types !duel name, a little pixel-art Old West town grows in along the bottom of your stream against a sunset. The two of them walk out of the saloon's swinging doors, stand back to back, take five paces, turn and draw. The winner soaks the loser, whose pistol only dribbles, and knocks their hat clean off. Nobody gets hurt. Then the whole town shrinks away, the sun sets, and there's nothing on screen until the next duel.

Setup guide

Get it on stream

  1. Add a Browser source In OBS click + under Sources → Browser. Paste https://duel.ixitxachitl.com/ as the URL and set it to your full canvas size, usually 1920 × 1080. The background is transparent automatically, and the town pops up in a small box at the bottom centre (or across the whole screen, if you'd rather: see Where it shows).
  2. Connect your chat Right-click the source → Interact, move your mouse over the overlay and click the yellow gear in the bottom-right corner. Type your Twitch and/or Kick channel name, add TikTok or YouTube if you stream there, and press Save & apply.
  3. Try it Test duel in the settings starts one between you and a pretend cowboy. The boxes next to the gear duel any two names you type, and Stop ends a duel early.
  4. Let chat duel Someone types !duel name. After the duel there's a cooldown (a minute by default) before the next one.
The settings window and the buttons show on stream while you're using them. Set things up before you go live.

Chat commands

  • !duel name: duels name (@name works too). They have to have chatted in the last 10 minutes, so nobody gets dragged into a duel when they're not around.
  • !duel on its own picks someone at random from the people who chatted in the last 10 minutes.
  • Everyone can start one by default. Who can start one in the settings changes that to mods and VIPs, mods, or just you, and you can let particular people too.
  • The cooldown starts when a duel ends. You skip it, and so can mods if you turn that on. While a duel is on, other !duels are ignored.

Chat sources

Twitch & Kick

Just type the channel name. Chat is read anonymously, so there's no login, bot account or app to set up. Each cowboy's shirt is their chat color, and mods and VIPs are recognised by their badges.

TikTok

TikTok needs a bridge app running on your PC. If you use TikFinity, just type 1 in the TikTok box. Otherwise, paste your bridge's ws://… address.

YouTube

Paste your live stream's video ID or link, plus a YouTube Data API v3 key from the Google Cloud Console. You can also give a bridge ws://… address instead.

What you'll see

  • A low-res pixel-art town against a sunset: a saloon with horses tied up out front, a jail, a bank, a hotel and a general store. Everything is a full 3D voxel model seen flat on, so it looks like 2D pixel art, with just the see-through ground showing some depth. It all twists and grows in, and shrinks back out at the end.
  • Two little cowboys or cowgirls in their chat colors. The same chatter always gets the same hat, hair and water pistol. Twitch chatters who have set their pronouns with the Chat Pronouns extension get a cowboy for he/him and a cowgirl for she/her; everyone else gets either.
  • Back to back, five paces (counted on screen), a turn, a tumbleweed, then DRAW!
  • A clear winner: one full stream of water, a soaked loser with their hat in the dirt and a puddle at their feet, and the winner's name in lights. No blood, nobody hurt.
  • Little sound effects (footsteps, a bell, the squirt and the splash), made on the fly. Turn them down or off in the settings.
